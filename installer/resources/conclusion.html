Installation complete

RecLight is ready to use. Follow these steps to finish setting it up:

  1. Install the plugin — done, you just completed this step.
  2. Connect the device — plug in the RecLight hardware and power it on.
  3. Connect to the RecLight Wi-Fi network — a window will open automatically, prompting you to enter your studio network's Wi-Fi details.
  4. Connect to your studio network — enter the credentials so the device joins your regular Wi-Fi.
  5. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW — so it picks up the newly installed RecLight plug-in.
  6. Add the RecLight plug-in to the master track — and you're ready to go.

Note: if an error message appears while entering the Wi-Fi details, wait a few seconds and continue the process — the device may just need a moment to respond.
